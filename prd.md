 Product Requirement Document (PRD): EcoSync v1.01.
 
  Executive Summary:
  EcoSync is an AI-native "Ghost Inventory" management system. It bridges the gap between consumer waste and the circular economy by using agentic AI to identify, value, and redistribute inactive personal assets. By linking digital receipts to real-time secondary markets, EcoSync transforms "clutter" into liquid capital and measurable carbon offsets.2. Experiential Outcomes (The "User Story")The "Aha!" Moment: The user connects their Gmail/Amazon account, and within seconds, a "$12,480.00" value appears—money they didn't know they "had" sitting in their closet or drawers.Agentic Proactivity: The system doesn't wait for the user. The Intelligence Feed pushes notifications: "Your iPhone 13 just hit its peak resale window before the next model launch. Sell now for $420."Frictionless Circularity: One-click transitions from "Owned" to "Listed for Sale," "Rented," or "Recycled."3. Functional RequirementsA. The Ghost Inventory Engine (Core Logic)Data Ingestion: Secure parsing of digital receipts and order confirmations (simulated via LLM-based regex/pattern matching).Asset Categorization: Automated tagging of items (Apparel, Tech, Home) and retrieval of original purchase price.Sustainability Health Index (SHI): A proprietary metric calculating the "Remaining Utility" of an item vs. its carbon footprint of replacement.B. The Intelligence Feed (Agentic Layer)Market Scraper: Real-time API calls to secondary markets (eBay, Back Market, Vestiaire Collective) to fetch "Floor Prices."Policy Integration: Real-time updates on circularity standards (e.g., EU Right to Repair, EPR tax credits).C. The Circular Matchmaker (Action Layer)LCA (Life Cycle Assessment) Viewer: Breakdown of the environmental cost to produce the specific item vs. the CO2 saved by extending its life.4.
